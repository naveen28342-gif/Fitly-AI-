    YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

    Privacy policy.

    A plain-language guide to what Fitly stores, why it is used, and the choices you have.

    PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY

    What we collect and why

    Last updated August 11, 2026

    Fitly uses the information you choose to provide to create practical workout and meal plans, remember your progress, and make the app useful across sessions. We do not sell your personal information.

    Signing out ends your current session. It does not delete your account data; when you sign in again, your saved profile, plans, and progress can be loaded for your account.

    NOTE: This website was built as part of my IBM x AICTE internship project. It is intended for educational and demonstration purposes only.

    1. Information you provide

    This may include your name and email from Google sign-in, age, height, weight, goals, diet preferences, equipment, health context, surgery or movement restrictions, check-ins, measurements, completed workouts, meal logs, grocery items, and chat messages.

    2. How we use it

    We use this information to personalize plans, calculate targets and progress summaries, save completion history, respond to your questions, and improve the reliability and safety of the experience.

    3. AI guidance

    When you ask for live guidance or generate a plan, the relevant question and plan context may be sent to the configured AI service provider so it can produce a response. Fitly does not use AI guidance as a diagnosis or medical treatment.

    4. Sign-in and storage

    Google handles the sign-in step. Fitly receives the account identity needed to create your account. Your profile, plans, and activity are stored with the app’s database service and are associated with your signed-in account so multiple users can keep separate progress.

    5. Your choices

    You can edit your profile, update preferences, log or uncheck activities, and sign out at any time. To request account-data correction or deletion, contact the person or organization operating this Fitly deployment before public launch.

    6. Health and safety

    Please do not use Fitly for emergencies or as a substitute for qualified medical advice. Stop exercising and seek professional help for concerning symptoms, injuries, surgery recovery, or health conditions.

    FITLY HELP CENTER

    How can we help?

    Quick answers for using your personal plan.

    How is my plan personalized?

    Your profile, goal, food preferences, available equipment, and training history shape future workout and meal plans. Use Edit profile or Generate new plan when something changes.

    When is a workout marked complete?

    Start the workout to run the timer. Mark each exercise complete as you finish it; Fitly records the workout only after every exercise is checked.

    How do I log food or groceries?

    Open Meal plans to mark planned meals complete or add what you ate manually. Open Grocery list to check off ingredients or add an item for your next shop.

    How is Progress calculated?

    Progress updates from completed workouts, meals you log, check-ins, and measurements you add. It reflects your own recorded activity, not a generic score.

    Health and safety

    Fitly is not a medical service. For injuries, surgery recovery, symptoms, or a health condition, pause and ask a qualified healthcare professional for advice.
